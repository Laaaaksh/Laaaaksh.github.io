hi

right now i’m building two things. vessel is a keyboard-driven tui for apple’s native mac containers. writ is for the other half of my day: agents work under a writ, and the tool checks whether they stayed inside it, then shows you only the drift. i’ve also built and open-sourced ten smaller tools, each one replacing a paid developer service, listed further down the page. all twelve are open source. if any of them sound interesting, come break them.

i’m also teaching myself cuda and gpu engineering, going a layer down from the services i’ve spent my career on, to where the arithmetic actually happens. i am very much a beginner at it and enjoying that.

i read a lot of philosophy, and i keep a running file of lines i don’t want to forget. the one i come back to most:

if your intentions are pure, not driven by ego, and if you truly want to do something great, you must accept that you may never get to see just how important you may become.

before this i led agentic engineering at snabbit, where i built the ai agents that carried work across our software development lifecycle: ticket intake, doc retrieval, pr review, even android and ios gui testing.

and before that, almost four years at razorpay writing go. i owned merchant experience end to end, onboarding a merchant through to getting them activated, and built the proxy that admin operations ran through.

i studied material science, with a computer science minor, at nit karnataka. still an unusual way to end up writing go for a living, and yet.

i also advise mounterra innovations on ai and technical direction. they’re a digital product development agency building ai-powered platforms, web and mobile apps, and saas products for startups and industrial companies.
